Composer's Key

Make the tune

The music at the top is today's tune: tap it to hear it. Set the board so one wave plays it, note for note, then tap Coda, bottom left, to send the wave up. It plays everything it meets, right or wrong, until it stops by itself; tap him again to stop it sooner. You can keep touching the board while it flies.

  • an arrow: tap to turn a drum, or wind a peg
  • a dial: tap to tune it
  • corners: drag it. A peg on a block slides along its string
  • nothing: it stays as it is

A string sounds when the wave crosses it. A longer string is lower; every wrap on a peg raises it a step. The little violins, trumpets and flutes play one note each. Drums bounce the wave and keep time; they are not the tune. Tap any instrument to hear it.
